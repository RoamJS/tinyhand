# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Added TinyHand for Roam. Point with a hand or laser dot, tap to highlight a spot, and draw freehand strokes and arrows over your graph.
- Undo one mark with the toolbar or **Cmd/Ctrl+Z**, clear all ink, and press **Escape** to return to the graph.
- The illustrated toolbar keeps gestures behind Help, offers all 15 original tap sounds, and remembers your pointer and sound choices in this browser.

- Toggle pointing and toolbar visibility from the command palette. Right-drag erases ink and Alt-click interacts with Roam. Interrupted drags keep their ink.
